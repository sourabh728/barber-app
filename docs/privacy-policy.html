Privacy Policy

Trimshim
Last updated: 28 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Trimshim (“we”, “our”, “the app”) collects and how we use it when you use our mobile app to find barbers, book appointments, or manage a shop.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number, and password (stored in hashed form).
  • Profile and shop photos: images you choose to upload for your profile or shop.
  • Booking information: appointment date/time, service, shop, staff selection, and related booking status.
  • Shop details (barbers): shop name, description, address, city, state, pincode, contact details, schedule, and staff information you enter.
  • Google Sign-In (optional): if you sign in with Google, we receive basic account information such as your name and email from Google.
  • Payment preference: your selected payment method preference (for example Cash, Card, or UPI) may be saved on your device. We do not collect full card numbers or UPI PINs in the app today.
  • Support messages: if you contact Help & Support, we receive the title and message you send, along with your account contact details so we can reply.

How we use your information

  • Create and manage your account
  • Send email verification and password-reset codes
  • Show shops, create and manage appointments
  • Display your profile or shop photo in the app
  • Respond to support requests
  • Improve reliability and security of the service

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only as needed to run the service, for example:

  • With the barber shop involved in your booking (name, phone, and appointment details)
  • With service providers that host our database, API, and email delivery
  • If required by law

Data storage and security

Account and booking data are stored on our servers and database providers. Passwords are stored hashed. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Data retention

We keep your account and booking information while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. You may request deletion of your account by contacting us.

Children

Trimshim is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

  • Update profile and shop details in the app
  • Choose whether to upload photos
  • Contact us to request account deletion or data correction

Changes
